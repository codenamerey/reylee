*This article is a reflection paper on Po-Shen Loh's blog, "[Why do we need mathematicians anymore?](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/09/19/why-do-we-need-human-mathematicians-anymore/)" following talks on the displacement of mathematicians from their jobs due to the latest capabilities of generative AI in the field.*

Most knowledge workers have worried about their job security due to the advancement of AI. I am one to talk: I am a software engineer who started his career at the break of 2024 back when code generators were mostly suggestive than agentic. By the beginning of 2025, the application of large-language models in programming has become more apparent: it started to do more and more of the coding, and by the year's dawn, I wasn't writing any code.

I felt angsty about the future of my work. After all, if AI can do my job fast and better, where is the need for a ticket-solving code monkey like me?
