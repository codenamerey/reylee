I love Itaewon Class. I watched the Korean drama at least twice, and read the manhwa once. It is a film about friendship, ambition, social justice, love, and the bit I liked most—integrity. IC has got to be my favorite drama, so much so that I can not watch Park Seo Joon play other roles save the stoic, blank-faced Park Saeroyi (I tried *Fight For My Way* as recommended by a friend—no, thank you.) Having read the Manhwa and the Kdrama adaptation, I've noticed quite some deviations and additions, that are **major, major, major**. Some of the details in the manhwa, I wish was retained. Some added details in the film, I quite liked. This here is a list of them.

>[!Caution]
> Obviously, this article contains spoilers, either from the Manhwa or the Kdrama series. If you wish to read the Manhwa (which I assume most have not), stop reading now!
> If you, conversely, don't care, and don't have much time to spare reading it, be my guest.
> If, on the flip side, you haven't watched the Kdrama series yet, well, what are you doing with your life!? Kidding aside, it's worth the watch.

### 1. There was never a Toni
In the manhwa, Toni, the Guinean guy who got hired for looking like a foreigner (but surprisingly, doesn't know English!), is non-existent. Which was too bad, because that also means that his grandma, Ms. Kim, who was like the series' [Fawkes](https://harrypotter.fandom.com/wiki/Fawkes), the link between Saeroyi and Chairman Jang, is also non-existent. There wasn't an investment fiasco that occured, no racist pubs, and no beautiful rooftop sing-along at the end, which is a bummer. I kind of liked Toni. Likely why there wasn't much of him by the end.

### 2. It didn't take years for Saeroyi to realize that he loves Yiseo back
Saeroyi's an absolute dunce. In the Kdrama series, he repeatedly refuses Yiseo's advances after Geun-won dropped the A bomb one drunken night that made everything between the lovebirds awkward. It takes, I think, three years (not sure about the figure) before Saeroyi realizes that he absolutely adores her manager after an epiphany. It made it seem like Yiseo grew on him, and that he eventually fell in love.

Except, that isn't what happened. Yes, it was an epiphany.

In the manhwa, it was actually Kwon Na-Ra (Saeroyi's first love, for you forgetful people) who made Saeroyi realize that he's got a thing for Yi-seo **all along!** Na-ra asked "who is the person that makes you the happiest" and was shocked to see a smiling Saeroyi
