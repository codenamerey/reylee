The mindful programmer

Hi! Name’s Lee. I have been programming since 2020 A.K.A le pre-AI times.

Writing is my first love. I used to be a writer for our school paper, The Kabyawan, back in high school. More so, I loved writing essays and poems, and I still sometimes do until today.

I am a programmer now mostly because I love to write. I think of programming as yet another medium for it. Given, you talk to a machine, but I always remember Uncle Bob’s adage: “code should read like beautifully-written prose”, which if you live by, takes programming closer to creative writing.

Anyhow, you can view some of my best works in the projects section, and my blog writing in here.

Thanks for staying for a short while.

Best, Lee Ryan
